Parotta

Description

Make classic South Indian Kerala Parotta (Malabar Parotta) at home with soft, flaky layers.

Ingredients

  • All-Purpose Flour
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Ghee or Oil
  • Water or Milk

Steps

  1. Make the Dough
  2. Shape into balls
  3. Roll and cut layers
  4. Roll lightly and cook
  5. Fluff the layers
